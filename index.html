05 · Under the hood

5 steps from raw image to final campaign, powered by the ORA Agent.

Step 1

Brand Memory

A persistent memory holds your products, guidelines, reference library and visual language.

Step 2

Analyse Product

Vision models read product geometry: stones, settings, textures and material properties.

Step 3

Understand User Intent

Intent extraction turns your hint into a creative direction, with gap checks that ask before assuming.

Step 4

Generate Image/Videos

Frontier model routing picks the best engine per job, with identity-locked generation keeping the product exact.

Step 5

Quality Check Output

An automated QA gate scores fidelity; an eval-and-repair loop fixes failures before delivery.
